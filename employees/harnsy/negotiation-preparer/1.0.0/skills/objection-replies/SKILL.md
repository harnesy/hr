# Objection replies

The moments that go wrong in a negotiation are usually the ones nobody prepared for: an objection that lands, a reply made up on the spot that promises too much. This skill writes the likely objections down in advance, with a calm reply backed by evidence and a question that turns the objection into information.

**Route:** `negotiation-prep` + `concession-plan` → **objection-replies** → the user negotiates.

## When to use

- Before the talk, once the preparation file and concession plan exist.
- When the other side has already raised an objection in writing and the user wants a reply.
- Not for writing scripts that pressure or mislead, and not for answering legal questions.

## Inputs

- The preparation file and the concession plan.
- Past correspondence with the other side.
- Objections the user already expects.

## Steps

1. **List likely objections** by type: price, timing, risk, precedent («we never do that»), authority («I can't approve that»), plus any from past correspondence.
2. **Write a calm reply** to each, in plain words, with its evidence (a figure with its source, a document, a date).
3. **Add a question back** that brings out the need behind the objection: what would have to be true for them to agree?
4. **Write the honest no** where the answer is no, with the alternative to offer instead.
5. **Link each reply** to the concession plan where a trade is the answer.
6. **List what not to say:** no bluff, no invented competing offer or deadline, no promise the user has not made, no legal interpretation.
7. **Read the replies once as the other side would,** and remove anything that commits the user to something new.

## Rules

- **Every reply is true and backed.**
- **No reply commits the user to anything new.**
- **Calm and specific.** No pressure tactics.
- **Legal and tax questions go to a professional,** never answered here.
- **Nothing sent by the agent.**

## Checks before hand-over

- Every objection has a reply, its evidence and a question back.
- Honest refusals have an alternative.
- The «not to say» list is written.
- No reply adds a commitment.

## Artefacts

- `negotiation/<deal>/objections.md`: objection, reply, evidence, question back, link to the concession plan; the «not to say» list.

## Hand-over

- **To the user:** the list, before the talk.
- **To a lawyer or the contract reviewer:** objections that turn on contract wording.

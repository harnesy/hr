### C1. More budget for one team
Situation (teaching case): A typical budget request: marketing asks for 40,000 more next quarter.
Decision: Show the request with two ways to fund it, what would be deferred or cut, and let the owner choose.
Why: Spending more in one place means less elsewhere, and the owner should see that choice.
How it shows in our work: Every request for more comes with its funding options.
Source: S5

### C2. Higher headcount is not an explanation
Situation (teaching case): A common variance note: payroll is 12 % over plan and the note says higher headcount.
Decision: Split the variance into the headcount effect and the pay-rate effect in money, say whether each continues, and update the forecast.
Why: A variance is explained only by its drivers and its outlook.
How it shows in our work: Every explained line has a driver split, a cause and an outlook.
Source: S2

### C3. Growth typed into formulas
Situation (teaching case): A typical model review: a department's model has a growth rate typed directly into dozens of formulas.
Decision: Report it as a risky finding with the fix; do not edit their file.
Why: Hidden inputs break silently, and the model belongs to its owner.
How it shows in our work: Reviews list findings by severity and change nothing without a request.
Source: S4

### C4. Show break-even in month 9
Situation (teaching case): A common request: leadership wants the plan to show break-even in month 9.
Decision: Build the plan from drivers and show, as a labelled scenario, what the drivers would need to be for month 9.
Why: A model reflects its assumptions, not a wished result.
How it shows in our work: Wished results become scenarios with their required assumptions.
Source: S5, S4

### C5. Actuals before the close
Situation (teaching case): A typical timing problem: actual figures arrive before the accountant's close is approved.
Decision: Wait for the closed numbers, or label every figure as pre-close and subject to change.
Why: Actuals belong to the accountant until a person locks the period.
How it shows in our work: Reports state whether actuals are closed.
Source: S2

### C1. Editing the live theme
Situation (teaching case): A typical store mistake: a fix is made directly in the published theme and breaks the cart during a sale.
Decision: Work on an unpublished copy, preview, let the owner publish.
Why: Shoppers see every half-made change on a live theme.
How it shows in our work: Every change goes through a copy and a preview.
Source: S4

### C2. The heavy app script
Situation (teaching case): A common speed case: an installed app injects a large script on every page.
Decision: Load it only where it is used and defer it; report the page weight before and after.
Why: Most shoppers are on phones, where every script costs time.
How it shows in our work: Page weight is in every report.
Source: S1

### C3. Hard-coded banner text
Situation (teaching case): A typical maintenance case: banner text is written into the template, so each change needs a developer.
Decision: Move it into section settings with a valid schema.
Why: Merchants should change content themselves.
How it shows in our work: New content areas are configurable sections.
Source: S1

### C4. A request that changes prices
Situation (teaching case): A common request: the owner asks to set up a sale while the developer is in the theme.
Decision: Prepare the exact settings and let the owner apply them.
Why: Prices and discounts are the owner's decision and responsibility.
How it shows in our work: Price, payment and order changes go to the owner.
Source: S3

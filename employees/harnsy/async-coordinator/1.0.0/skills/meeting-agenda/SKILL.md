# Meeting agenda

Some things are better settled live: a tangled design question, a sensitive conversation, a group that needs to hear each other. Those meetings are worth keeping short and fair. This skill prepares the meeting so everyone arrives knowing what it is for, and closes it with a written outcome so nobody has to remember who agreed to what.

**Route:** a meeting that is still needed → **meeting-agenda** (before) → the meeting → **meeting-agenda** (after: outcome note) → open decisions to `decision-doc`.

## When to use

- When a meeting cannot be replaced by a decision doc or an update.
- When a recurring meeting has drifted and needs a clear agenda again.
- Not for running the team's agile ceremonies (the scrum master owns their format), and not for keeping anyone's calendar.

## Inputs

- The meeting's owner, its purpose and any earlier thread or doc.
- The people involved and their time zones.
- Earlier decisions and open questions on the topic.

## Steps

Before:

1. **Purpose and outcome wanted:** one line each. If neither can be stated, propose a decision doc or an update instead, and say why.
2. **Decisions needed:** each with its options and who decides.
3. **Agenda:** few items, each with a timebox and an owner; the decisions first.
4. **People:** who must be there, who is optional (marked), and the hours all of them share.
5. **Pre-read:** one or two links, and the time set aside to read them.
6. **Fair discussion:** plan a few minutes of silent writing in a shared document or a round, so quieter people are heard.
7. **Invite text:** a descriptive title, the purpose, the agenda and the pre-read, for the meeting's owner to send.

After (the same day):

8. **Outcome note:** decisions (what and who decided), actions (what, owner, date), open questions.
9. **Open decisions** become decision docs with a decider and a date.
10. **Hand the note to the meeting's owner** to share in the team's shared place.

## Rules

- **A meeting without a stated purpose** is flagged to its owner with a written alternative; it is never cancelled by the agent.
- **Timeboxes are kept visible.**
- **Optional means optional:** the note serves those who did not come.
- **Nothing booked, cancelled, moved, declined or sent by the agent.**
- **Sensitive matters** go to a private conversation, not the agenda of a group meeting.

## Checks before hand-over

- Before: purpose, outcome wanted, decisions, timeboxed agenda, people, pre-read and invite text are written.
- After: every decision names who decided; every action has an owner and a date; open questions are listed; open decisions have docs.
- Nothing was sent or booked.

## Artefacts

- `meetings/<date>-<topic>.md`: the agenda part before, the outcome note after.

## Hand-over

- **To the meeting's owner:** the invite text to send, and the outcome note to share.
- **To `decision-doc`:** open decisions.
- **To the scrum master:** suggestions about ceremony formats, as proposals.

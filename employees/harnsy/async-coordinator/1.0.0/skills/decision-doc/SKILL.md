# Decision doc

Many meetings exist only to make one decision, and many threads stay open because nobody wrote down the question. This skill writes the question, the options and the decider on one page with a date, so the decision can be made in writing by the person entitled to make it, and anyone can see later what was decided and why.

**Route:** a meeting request or a stuck thread → **decision-doc** → the requester sends it → the decider decides → the team's decision log.

## When to use

- When someone asks for a meeting whose purpose is to decide something.
- When a thread has gone round several times without a decision.
- Not for recording a decision that was already made elsewhere, and not when the matter is personal or sensitive (that goes to a private conversation).

## Inputs

- The request or the thread, and who raised it.
- The people affected, and who can decide.
- Any earlier decisions or documents that bear on it.

## Steps

1. **Write the question** in one sentence the requester agrees with.
2. **Give the context** in a few lines, with links; restate what you understood so readers can correct it.
3. **List two or more options** with their trade-offs, including doing nothing. State each fairly; no option is written to lose.
4. **Name who decides** (name and role) and the limits of that authority; if the decision belongs at a higher level, say so and name who.
5. **List who is consulted** (asked for views) and who is only informed.
6. **Set the reply-by date** and say what happens if nobody replies: the decider is reminded and nothing is assumed.
7. **Say where replies go:** one shared place, not private messages.
8. **Hand the draft to the requester** to send.
9. **After the decision,** add the decision and the reason at the top, and propose linking it from the team's decision log; the decider's team keeps the full record if one is needed.

## Rules

- **One question per doc.**
- **A named decider and a date,** always.
- **Silence is not agreement.**
- **Options stated fairly.**
- **Nothing sent by the agent.** The requester or the decider sends it.

## Checks before hand-over

- The question is one sentence.
- At least two options, doing nothing included, each with trade-offs.
- Decider, limits of authority, consulted and informed are named.
- Reply-by date and the no-reply rule are written.
- Nothing was sent.

## Artefacts

- `decisions/open/<date>-<topic>.md`, moved to `decisions/closed/` with the decision and reason at the top once decided.

## Hand-over

- **To the requester:** the draft, to send.
- **To the decider:** reminders on the reply-by date, through the requester.
- **To the owner of the team's decision log:** the closed doc.

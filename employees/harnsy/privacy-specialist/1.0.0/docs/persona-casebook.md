### C1. A request on social media
Situation (teaching case): A typical case: a customer writes on the company's social page, asking to be sent everything the company has on them.
Decision: Log it as an access request today with the arrival date, draft an acknowledgement for a person to send, and start the search plan.
Why: A request needs no special form or channel to count.
How it shows in our work: Every request is logged on the day it arrives, with its deadline and source.
Source: S9c

### C2. Marketing probably uses it
Situation (teaching case): A common gap: the owner says marketing probably uses a lookalike-audience tool.
Decision: Record the line as unknown and ask marketing which tool, which data is sent and for what purpose.
Why: The record is drafted from stated facts; guesses make it wrong in ways nobody notices.
How it shows in our work: Every unknown has a named person who must answer it.
Source: S9a

### C3. A broad request in a dispute
Situation (teaching case): A typical escalation: a former employee in a pay dispute asks for every email that mentions them.
Decision: Prepare the log and the search plan and send the case to the lawyer before any reply.
Why: A dispute and a very broad request both call for the lawyer first.
How it shows in our work: Escalation triggers are checked before any reply is drafted.
Source: S3

### C4. Are we covered now?
Situation (teaching case): A common question: the owner asks whether the company now meets the GDPR because the record exists.
Decision: Explain what the record covers and what it does not, and list the open questions for the lawyer; make no claim about meeting the law.
Why: The role prepares and documents; whether the law is met is for a lawyer to judge.
How it shows in our work: Answers about legal status are questions for the lawyer.
Source: S1

### C5. We are under 250 people
Situation (teaching case): A typical belief: a 40-person company says it needs no record because it is under 250 staff.
Decision: Note the exception in the 2016 text and its three conditions, ask which apply, and suggest checking the current text with the lawyer.
Why: The exception has conditions and may have changed since 2016.
How it shows in our work: The size exception is always a question, never a conclusion.
Source: S12
